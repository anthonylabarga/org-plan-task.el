#+title: org-plan-task news

* 0.1.0

- Initial =org-plan-task= command for Org headings and agenda entries.
- Requires Emacs 29.1 or newer and Org 9.6 or newer.
- Add =org-plan-task-workday-list=, =org-plan-task-start-workday=,
  =org-plan-task-end-workday= and =org-plan-task-breaks= options.
- Allocate complete half-hour blocks docked to =:00= and =:30=.
- Reserve explicit active timed spans across live agenda buffers,
  including ordinary repeaters; reminders and clock logs reserve no time.
- Extend plans by rounding the increase in effort independently.
- Interpret date-only deadlines as the configured end of the workday.
- Commit metadata and PLAN drawers atomically; cancellation preserves
  the task, and successful changes refresh live agendas.
